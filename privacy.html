Privacy Policy

Ultimate Albanian Flashcards · Last updated 10 November 2026

In short: the app collects nothing about you. Your progress and voice recordings stay on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Who we are

Ultimate Albanian Flashcards (“the app”) is published by the developer shown on its Google Play page. Questions about this policy: hello@lysco.co.uk.

What stays on your device

  • Study progress (which cards you have studied, your answers, settings, flags). It is stored only in the app’s private storage on your phone. We never receive it.
  • Voice recordings. The microphone is used only when you tap Record, so you can compare your pronunciation with the native audio. A recording exists only in your phone’s memory while that card is on screen and is discarded when you move on. It is never saved to storage or sent anywhere.
  • Backups you export. If you use Settings → Export, the app creates a file on your device. Where that file goes is up to you.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no tracking. It does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment details under the Google Privacy Policy. The app only asks Google Play whether your subscription is active; we never see your payment details.

Android backup

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app’s study progress in your device backup, so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is stored by Google under your account and controlled in your phone’s settings. We cannot access it.

Permissions

  • Microphone: only for the pronunciation recorder, and only after you allow it.
  • Internet: only to check your subscription with Google Play. All words and audio are built into the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it stored on your phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
